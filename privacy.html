Privacy Policy

Your screen stays on your Mac.

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Applies to Copyshape for Mac 1.0.1 and later

🔒

Copyshape doesn't collect any data.No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app never sends what you capture anywhere.

What happens when you capture

  • When you press the shortcut and draw a box, Copyshape takes one image of only that box, using macOS ScreenCaptureKit. It never records your screen in the background.
  • The text in that image is read on your Mac by Apple's built-in Vision framework. The image is not saved and is discarded as soon as the text is read.
  • The text is placed on your clipboard, where you paste it. Copyshape doesn't keep a copy.

Screen Recording permission

macOS calls reading pixels on screen "Screen Recording", so Copyshape asks for that permission. It uses it only for the box you draw, only when you ask. You can turn it off any time in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Screen & System Audio Recording.

What's stored on your Mac

  • Your settings, such as shortcuts, default copy format and popup position.
  • On the free plan, a count of today's formatted captures, kept in your Mac's Keychain so the daily limit works.

This stays on your Mac. Deleting the app removes its settings.

Purchases

Copyshape Pro is bought through the Mac App Store. Apple handles the payment; Copyshape never sees your payment details, name or email. The app checks your purchase on your Mac with Apple's StoreKit.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in macOS settings, Apple may send anonymous crash reports. These contain technical details about the crash, not your captures.

Children

Copyshape doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a future feature ever needs to send data (for example, an optional cloud mode), it will be off by default and explained here first.
