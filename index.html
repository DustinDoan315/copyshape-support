    Good to know

    Does Copyshape upload my screen?

    No. Text is read on your Mac with Apple's Vision framework, even offline. It only looks inside the box you draw.

    Why does macOS ask for Screen Recording?

    That's what macOS calls reading pixels on screen. Copyshape uses it only for the area you capture.
