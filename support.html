Getting started

How do I copy text?

Press ⌥⇧C (or click the dashed-square icon in the menu bar and choose Capture Zone), then drag a box over the text. Release, and it's on your clipboard. Paste with ⌘V.

Press Space instead of dragging to capture a whole window, and ⌥⇧R to capture the same box again.

Copyshape says Screen Recording is off
  1. Open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Screen & System Audio Recording.
  2. Turn on Copyshape. If it isn't listed, click + and choose Copyshape from Applications.
  3. When macOS asks, click Quit & Reopen.

Still off after updating? Select Copyshape in that list, click −, then add it again.

The shortcut does nothing

Another app may use the same shortcut. Open Copyshape Settings (⌘, from the menu-bar menu) and record a different one. Settings warns you when a shortcut is taken.

Results

Some text came out wrong
  • Zoom in or make the text bigger, then capture again. Small or blurry text is harder to read.
  • Draw the box tightly around the text, leaving out toolbars and icons.
  • Want each screen line kept as its own line? Turn on "Keep line breaks" in Settings.
It pasted in the wrong format

Use Copy as in the result popup: Markdown, CSV or Plain text. You can also set a default in Settings ▸ Copying.

"This app blocks screen capture"

Some apps, such as streaming services, protect their content and capture as black. Copyshape doesn't bypass this.

Copyshape Pro

What does Pro include?

Unlimited formatted captures (tables, lists, code) and every Copy as format. Plain text is always unlimited, even on the free plan. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

I bought Pro but the app still shows the free plan

Open Settings and click Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account in the App Store that you used to buy Pro.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
