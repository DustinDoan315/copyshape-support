Using Copyshape

  • Copyshape reads text from the area of your screen you choose and puts it on your clipboard.
  • You're responsible for what you capture and how you use it. Respect copyright and the terms of the apps and content you capture.
  • Copyshape doesn't bypass protected content. Apps that block screen capture stay blocked.

Free plan and Copyshape Pro

  • The free plan includes unlimited plain text and a limited number of formatted captures per day.
  • Copyshape Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that removes the daily limit. It's not a subscription.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Pro works on every Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. Use "Restore Purchase" in Settings on a new Mac.

Accuracy

Text recognition is very good but not perfect, especially with small, blurry or low-contrast text. Check important text, such as numbers, codes and amounts, before you rely on it.
